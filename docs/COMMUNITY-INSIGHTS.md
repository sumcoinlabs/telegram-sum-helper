## Editorial policy

The export covers different network eras. Old member comments and even old project announcements are **historical context**, not current protocol rules, live service status or authoritative personal financial advice. Only carefully worded FAQs with current official source references enter `knowledge/curated.json`. No raw user posts, Telegram IDs, or private group-history data are committed.
